Client Handover & Pre-Flight Protocol

Launch Acceptance & Verification Guide

This document outlines the verified items, required client sign-offs, technical domain fixes, and content production milestones required before Wood N Work’s digital showroom goes live.

Urgent Action Required Section 02 & 15 Finding

1. Domain SSL Certificate Date Error (`woodnwork.in`)

Investigation Finding: Opening woodnwork.in returned an SSL certificate-date / expiration error in browser security checks. Visitors attempting to open the URL encounter a full-screen browser security warning block.

Resolution Steps for Domain Owner:
  1. Log into the domain registrar / DNS management console (e.g. GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare).
  2. Renew the TLS/SSL certificate or re-issue an automated Let's Encrypt / Cloudflare Universal SSL certificate.
  3. Verify HTTPS enforcement and HTTP-to-HTTPS 301 redirection on the production server.
Client Confirmation Needed Section 02 Contact Audit

2. Telephone & WhatsApp Confirmation (+91 9090 9999 16)

Investigation Finding: The Instagram bio lists +91 9090 9999 16. Per brief guidelines: “confirm the number with the business before using it in clickable phone or WhatsApp links... Do not activate phone or WhatsApp links until the business confirms the number.”

Current Prototype Implementation:

The number is presented transparently with an explanatory disclaimer pill. Direct click-to-call links are guarded, and WhatsApp actions prompt a confirmation modal until client sign-off is logged.

Editorial Standard Enforced Section 01 Positioning

3. "35+ Years Experience" Heritage Phrasing

Instruction: “Describe that as experience in timber or plywood unless the business confirms the exact history. Do not imply that Wood N Work’s modular-furniture brand has existed for 35 years.”

✓ Verified Implementation: All site pages describe this as “35+ years of family timber and plywood knowledge” and explicitly position Wood N Work as the modern factory-direct bespoke modular brand in Nashik.
