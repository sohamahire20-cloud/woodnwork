Roots & Responsibility

35+ years of timber knowledge.
Direct from our factory floor.

Wood N Work is rooted in over three decades of family heritage in timber and plywood trading and processing. We understand the biology of wood, moisture behaviour across Indian seasons, core board stability, and veneer grading at a cellular level.

Editorial Clarification: While our founding family brings 35+ years of timber trade background, Wood N Work operates as a modern bespoke modular brand in Nashik with direct factory production—eliminating showroom middlemen markups while maintaining total quality oversight.

The Consultation Journey

A structured, considered process.

From the first site conversation to the final handover, our team guides you through transparent design, material curation, and factory execution.

01

Spatial Dialogue

We review your architectural floor plan, discuss your cooking and storage habits, and identify functional zones for your lifestyle.

02

Material & 3D Curation

Review tactile timber samples, handle-less profile details, quartz surfaces, and visual 3D design configurations tailored to your space.

03

Factory Production

Precision CNC cutting, zero-joint edge-banding, and pre-assembly testing occur inside our factory before components leave for site.

04

White-Glove Installation

Our dedicated Nashik installation carpenters fit each modular unit with laser alignment, soft-close calibration, and clean handover.
