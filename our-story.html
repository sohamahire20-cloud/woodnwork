Heritage & Workshop Philosophy

Material knowledge. Made personal.

Wood N Work represents the union of multi-generational timber knowledge and modern factory-direct modular engineering in Nashik. We build considered spaces for homeowners who care about what lies beneath the surface.

Our Roots

35+ years of timber & plywood understanding.

Long before modern modular CNC machinery, our founding family was immersed in the timber and plywood trade. Over 35+ years, we developed a tactile, foundational understanding of natural timber: how humidity shifts across Indian seasons affect expansion, which core board grades resist delamination, and how veneer grain flows across continuous joinery.

In creating Wood N Work, we took that raw timber foundation and directed it into contemporary residential manufacturing in Nashik. Rather than buying pre-cut flat-pack cabinets from distant third parties, we set up our own workshop to fabricate custom furniture directly.

Brand Integrity Standard:

Our 35+ years of experience reflects our family’s deep roots in timber and plywood trading and processing. Wood N Work operates as a modern bespoke modular brand in Nashik, maintaining direct factory control over every project.

Why Factory Direct Matters

No middlemen. No compromises.

When you purchase modular furniture through a retail franchise, your budget is split between dealer markups, showroom rents, and sub-contracted installation crews. We do things differently.

01

Direct Price-to-Quality Ratio

Your investment goes entirely into certified BWP marine plywood, high-grade veneers, and precision hardware—not middleman commissions.

02

Custom Millimeter Tolerances

Franchise dealers must stick to standard 300/450/600mm box modules. Because we operate the factory, we cut cabinets to the exact millimeter of your room.

03

Single Point of Accountability

The same Nashik team that consults on your space oversees fabrication and carries out the final installation. There are no passing-the-buck excuses.

How We Work

From initial dialogue to final handover.

We operate a structured, consultation-led journey that ensures you understand every material choice, internal drawer layout, and schedule milestone.

01

Spatial Consultation

We review your architectural plans or visit your residence in Nashik. We discuss your cooking style, storage priorities, and aesthetic preferences.

02

Technical 3D & Sampling

We prepare millimeter-accurate shop drawings and review physical tactile timber veneers, super-matt acrylics, and hardware configurations together.

03

Factory Production

Components are precision cut, edge-banded with moisture-resistant glue, and dry-assembled inside our workshop for strict quality inspection.

04

White-Glove Installation

Our dedicated Nashik installation carpenters fit your units, laser-align doors, calibrate soft-close runners, and conduct a thorough handover.

The Craft Team

Dedicated to enduring craft in Nashik.

Behind every modular run is a dedicated team of spatial designers, CNC technicians, and master carpenters who take personal pride in the finished result.
