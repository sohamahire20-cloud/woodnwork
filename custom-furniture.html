Factory-Direct Custom Joinery

Made-to-order furniture built for your exact architecture.

From floor-to-ceiling wardrobe suites to floating slatted credenzas, Wood N Work manufactures custom residential joinery in Nashik. We build to your room’s exact millimeter rather than forcing standard catalog modules.

Confirmed Categories

Bespoke storage and living joinery.

Confirmed Specialism

Bespoke Floor-to-Ceiling Wardrobes

Designed to utilise vertical wall space completely, avoiding dust-collecting gaps atop the cabinet. Offered in hinged, sliding, or walk-in configurations with integrated lighting.

  • Custom heights up to 3.2m with zero visible sagging
  • Fluted tinted glass, book-matched natural veneers, or super-matt acrylics
  • Tailored internal configurations for ethnic wear, sarees, suits, and accessories
Confirmed Specialism

Floating Credenzas & Media Consoles

Wall-hung low-profile consoles engineered with heavy-duty internal brackets to float effortlessly. Houses audio-visual components, soundbars, and gaming consoles without exposed wires.

  • Concealed acoustic fabric doors or CNC-routed slatted timber
  • Internal ventilation channels to prevent electronics overheating
  • Chamfered 45-degree mitred edge joinery
Confirmed Specialism

Architectural Timber Panelling

Full-height wall panelling, hidden flush-door integration, and slatted room dividers that create visual rhythm and acoustic dampening across living and dining areas.

  • Concealed secret doors flush with adjacent wall cladding
  • Solid timber battens over moisture-stable plywood backing
  • Seamless architectural transitions between spaces
Confirmed Specialism

Dining Buffets & Custom Bar Units

Tailored dining storage with dedicated felt-lined silverware drawers, wine stemware racks, internal LED display niches, and stone or brass service shelves.

  • Pocket doors that tuck away flush during hosting
  • Integrated refrigeration cutouts and power junctions
  • Hand-finished oil or polyurethane timber protection
The Customisation Framework

Options the business actually provides.

We avoid unnecessary complexity. Here is how Wood N Work personalises each piece of furniture to fit your lifestyle and interior architecture.

Millimeter Dimensions

Every cabinet is fabricated to your specific wall dimensions. We eliminate standard filler strips, so your wardrobes fit seamlessly wall-to-wall and floor-to-ceiling.

Internal Compartments

Choose your drawer depths, hanging heights (long ethnic gowns vs shirts), pull-out shoe carousels, and velvet watch and jewellery inserts.

Material Curation

Select from American walnut, teak, smoked oak, anti-scratch matte laminates, or fluted glass profiles. We ensure grain continuity across continuous doors.
