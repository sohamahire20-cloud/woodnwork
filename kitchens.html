Factory-Direct Kitchen Engineering

Kitchens crafted around how you actually cook.

We don’t assemble predetermined flat-packs. Wood N Work custom manufactures modular kitchens in Nashik to the exact millimeter of your room, pairing heavy-duty marine-grade cores with architectural veneers, acrylics, and ergonomics.

Spatial Typologies

Considered kitchen layouts for Nashik homes.

Every culinary space requires a distinct ergonomic balance between prep zones, cooking hobs, washing stations, and daily pantry accessibility.

Verified Typology

The Parallel (Galley) Kitchen

Widely regarded by culinary professionals as the most efficient spatial configuration. It creates two opposing counters—allowing clean separation between a wet prep/wash run and a dry cooking/appliance bank.

  • Ideal for medium-to-large apartments and dedicated cooking zones
  • Clear 1,200mm passage for effortless two-person movement
  • Zero dead-corner space: 100% accessible drawer runs
Verified Typology

The Open Island Kitchen

Designed for social residences and open-plan living. A central freestanding island provides expansive prep room, deep pot drawers, and an integrated casual breakfast bar overlooking the living area.

  • Combines social dining with high-capacity internal joinery
  • Can accommodate under-counter wine storage or prep sink
  • Creates a natural architectural focal point for the residence
Verified Typology

The L-Shaped Kitchen

Maximises floor space in corner configurations while maintaining a classic ergonomic working triangle between the refrigerator, sink, and cooktop. Leaves ample room for a dining table.

  • Adaptable to compact and open-concept floor plans alike
  • Fitted with smooth blind-corner pull-outs to utilize every corner
  • Enables uninterrupted sightlines into dining spaces
Verified Typology

The U-Shaped Workroom

The ultimate configuration for high-density storage and extensive countertop area. Surrounds the cook on three sides with continuous worktop and floor-to-ceiling larder cabinetry.

  • Maximum storage capacity and unbroken counter runs
  • Dedicated zones for small appliances and spices
  • Ideal for private, gourmet cooking environments

Consultation Preparation

What to consider before your consultation.

Having clarity on these elements helps us prepare an accurate preliminary spatial layout and material recommendation for your space.

01

Cooking Habits & Work Triangle

Do you require heavy tempering and extensive wet prep? A parallel galley or dedicated spice zone prevents grease spread to social living spaces.

02

Appliances & Electrical Cutouts

Make a note of your refrigerator dimensions, built-in microwave/oven specs, and dishwasher or water purifier positions so carcasses are custom sized.

03

Countertop Preference

We coordinate joinery with your chosen worktop—whether natural Indian granite, engineered quartz, or sintered stone.
